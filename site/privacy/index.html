Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

ytwchat is built so that we cannot read what you send. This policy explains what we can see, what we keep, for how long, and why.

What we never have

The contents of your messages, voice notes, photos, files and calls are end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted on your device and can only be decrypted on the devices of the people in the conversation. We never have the keys, so we cannot read, scan or hand over that content.

What we collect

  • Account details: your username, display name, email address, a securely hashed version of your password, and your phone number if you choose to add one.
  • Profile photo and display name, which people you chat with can see.
  • If you sign in with YTW: your YTW account ID, name and email address, provided by YTW.
  • Devices: the type of each device you sign in on, its public encryption keys, and a push notification token so we can tell it a new message has arrived.
  • Delivery information: which conversations you are in, and when encrypted messages are sent and delivered, so we can route them to the right devices.
  • Security logs: IP addresses and sign-in events, used to protect accounts and stop abuse.

How long we keep it

  • Encrypted messages are deleted from our servers once every device in the conversation has received them, and after 30 days at most.
  • Encrypted photos, voice notes and files are deleted from our servers after 30 days.
  • Security logs with IP addresses are deleted after 30 days.
  • Account details are kept until you delete your account.

Finding your contacts

If you allow it, ytwchat checks the phone numbers in your contacts to show which of them use ytwchat. The numbers are compared against registered accounts and then discarded. We do not store your contact list.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads or use your information for advertising.
  • This website uses no trackers, analytics cookies or third-party fonts.

Who helps us run ytwchat

We use a small number of service providers: our server hosting provider, Apple and Google to deliver push notifications (they only see that a notification was sent, never the message), and an email provider for account emails such as password resets. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.

Your choices and rights

You can change your profile, remove your phone number, sign out devices and delete your account in the app. Deleting your account removes your account details and any encrypted data we still hold for you. You can also ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to correct it, by emailing privacy@ytwchat.com.

Children

ytwchat is not for children under 13, or the minimum age set by the law where you live if that is higher.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@ytwchat.com
