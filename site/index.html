How your messages stay private

  1. Your phone makes the keys

    When you sign up, ytwchat creates encryption keys on your phone. The private key never leaves it.

  2. Messages are locked before they leave

    Every message, voice note and photo is encrypted on your phone. Our servers only ever pass along locked data.

  3. Only their phone can unlock it

    The person you are chatting with unlocks it on their phone. Nobody in between can read it, including us.

ytwchat uses MLS (Messaging Layer Security, RFC 9420), the open standard for secure messaging, built for groups and for people with more than one device.

Questions

Can ytwchat read my messages?

No. Messages, voice notes, photos and calls are encrypted on your phone and only unlocked on the phones of the people in the chat. Our servers only pass along locked data and never have the keys.

Do I need a YTW account?

No. You can sign up with your email address. If you already use YTW, you can sign in with your YTW account instead.

What does ytwchat store about me?

Your account details, the devices you use, and who you chat with and when, so messages can be delivered. Never the contents of your messages, voice notes, photos or calls. The privacy policy has the full list.
